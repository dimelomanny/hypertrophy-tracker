How the plan works

  • Split: D1 Push, D2 Pull, D3 Legs (quad focus), D4 Upper, D5 Lower (glute/hamstring focus). Every muscle about twice a week.
  • Volume: about 10-20 hard sets per muscle per week, mostly 6-15 reps, 1-3 reps short of failure.
  • Weekly rotation: Weeks 1-4 swap angles and equipment for the same muscles.
  • No decline bench: each Push and Upper day has one decline substitute: high-to-low cable fly, DB hip-bridge press, Smith hip-bridge floor press, or standing cable decline press. Abs use cable crunches or leg raises off the flat bench end.
  • Progression: 3 RIR, then 2, then 1-2, then 1 with an extra set on the first lift. After week 4, take an optional easy week, then restart week 1 and beat your "Last time" numbers.
  • Rest: 2-3 min on heavy compounds, 60-90 s on isolation work.
Eating to grow: to add muscle, aim for a modest calorie surplus (roughly 250-500 kcal a day above maintenance) and about 1.6-2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight a day (0.7-1 g per lb). Target about 0.5-1 lb of gain a month. General guidance, not medical advice.

Calories are an estimate: body weight (kg) × 6.0 MET × hours in sets, plus × 1.75 MET for rest and the rest of the session. Progress is saved on this device; use Export backup now and then.
